# Image credits

Every image in `src/assets/images/` must have a row here before the project is shared. Rows stay empty until an image is downloaded and approved.

## Licence notes

- **Unsplash License** and **Pexels License**: free to download, use and modify, no attribution required (we credit anyway). Both **forbid redistributing the photos as stock** (selling them, or offering them as a standalone/competing photo library). Bundling them inside this app is fine; do not publish the raw image folder as a downloadable photo pack. Unsplash+ (paid) images are **not** allowed.
- **Pixabay Content License**: free use, no attribution required, no redistribution of unaltered files as stock. Not CC0.
- **Wikimedia Commons**: only **CC0 / Public Domain** or **CC-BY** (credit required, shown below and in the app credits). No CC-BY-SA, no NC, no ND.
- Images are resized/cropped to 512x512 (modification); the originals' licences still apply.
- Do not use photos with identifiable people, watermarks, text or brands.
